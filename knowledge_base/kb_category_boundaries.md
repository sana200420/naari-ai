# NaariAI — Knowledge Base Category Boundaries
**2000-FAQ knowledge base · 8 subtopics × 250 FAQs · 4 owners**

This is the rulebook for where a question goes. Read your own two subtopics' "Excludes" lines before drafting — most duplication happens at these exact seams. When a question could plausibly sit in two places, the **owning subtopic listed here wins**; don't also write it into the other one.

---

## Sabiha — Menstrual Health & Periods

**Covers:** normal cycle length and flow, menarche (first period), period pain/cramps, PMS symptoms, heavy or light bleeding *not* caused by pregnancy/postpartum/PCOS/perimenopause, menstrual hygiene (pad/cloth use, changing frequency, disposal), cycle tracking, irregular periods from ordinary causes (stress, travel, weight change, puberty, breastfeeding-related delay).

**Excludes → goes elsewhere:**
- Irregular/absent periods suspected to be PCOS or another hormonal disorder → *PCOS & Hormonal Health*
- Irregular periods in a woman 40+ likely due to perimenopause → *Menopause & Menopausal Health*
- Missed period as a possible pregnancy sign → *Pregnancy & Maternal Health*
- Vaginal discharge, odor, itching, infections → *Vaginal & Personal Hygiene*
- Emotional/mood impact of period symptoms (mood swings, irritability as a feeling, not the biology) → *Mental Health & Emotional Well-being*

**Suggested sub-clusters (~30–35 FAQs each):** cycle basics & what's normal · menarche & puberty · period pain & cramp relief · flow changes (heavy/light/spotting) · menstrual hygiene practices & products · cycle tracking & irregularity from ordinary causes · myths & cultural taboos around periods

## Sabiha — Mental Health & Emotional Well-being

**Covers:** everyday stress and anxiety, mood, coping strategies, self-care, sleep and emotional wellbeing, family/relationship stress, body image, the *emotional* experience of baby blues/postpartum depression, emotional impact of menstrual/menopausal/PCOS symptoms, when to seek mental health support, stigma around mental health.

**Excludes → goes elsewhere:**
- Physical postpartum recovery, physical symptoms of baby blues timeline → *Pregnancy & Maternal Health*
- Physical symptoms of PCOS, menopause, or period pain themselves → their respective subtopics
- General physical wellness (sleep hygiene as a body topic, exercise) → *Women's Nutrition & Wellness*

**Suggested sub-clusters:** everyday stress & anxiety · mood & emotional coping · postpartum emotional wellbeing (baby blues/PPD — feelings only) · body image & self-esteem · family & relationship stress · sleep & mental wellbeing · when and how to seek help · stigma & talking about mental health

## Sana — Pregnancy & Maternal Health

**Covers:** trimester-by-trimester care, antenatal check-up schedule, pregnancy symptoms/discomforts, pregnancy-specific nutrition (iron-folic acid, foods to avoid), labor and delivery signs, where/how to give birth, physical postpartum recovery, breastfeeding and newborn basics, pregnancy and postpartum danger signs.

**Excludes → goes elsewhere:**
- Trying to conceive, ovulation timing, infertility → *Fertility & Reproductive Health*
- General (non-pregnancy) diet and nutrition → *Women's Nutrition & Wellness*
- Emotional/mood side of baby blues or postpartum depression → *Mental Health & Emotional Well-being*
- Birth spacing / contraception after delivery → *Fertility & Reproductive Health*

**Suggested sub-clusters:** confirming & early pregnancy · trimester-by-trimester care · antenatal visits & tests · pregnancy discomforts & symptoms · pregnancy-specific nutrition (iron/folic acid, foods) · labor, delivery & where to give birth · physical postpartum recovery · breastfeeding & newborn basics · pregnancy/postpartum danger signs

## Sana — PCOS & Hormonal Health

**Covers:** PCOS symptoms, diagnosis, and management, hormonal imbalance generally (thyroid effects on cycle/weight, insulin resistance), acne/excess hair growth/weight changes linked to hormones, hormone-driven irregular or absent periods.

**Excludes → goes elsewhere:**
- Ordinary period irregularity with no suspected hormonal cause → *Menstrual Health & Periods*
- Choice of hormonal contraception as a family-planning method → *Fertility & Reproductive Health*
- Perimenopause/menopause hormonal changes → *Menopause & Menopausal Health*
- Emotional impact of PCOS (body image, mood) → *Mental Health & Emotional Well-being*

**Suggested sub-clusters:** what PCOS is & common symptoms · diagnosis & when to get checked · PCOS and periods · PCOS, weight & insulin resistance · skin & hair changes (acne, excess hair) · thyroid & other hormonal imbalances · lifestyle management · myths about PCOS/hormones

## Tooba — Fertility & Reproductive Health

**Covers:** trying to conceive, ovulation and fertile-window tracking, infertility (causes, when to seek help), contraception and family planning methods, birth spacing, reproductive organ anatomy/function, STIs and reproductive tract infections *as they relate to fertility*.

**Excludes → goes elsewhere:**
- Routine intimate hygiene, non-fertility-related discharge/itching/odor → *Vaginal & Personal Hygiene*
- Confirmed pregnancy care → *Pregnancy & Maternal Health*
- PCOS as a hormonal condition (beyond "this can affect fertility") → *PCOS & Hormonal Health*

**Suggested sub-clusters:** how conception works & fertile window · trying to conceive · infertility causes & when to seek help · contraception & family planning methods · birth spacing · reproductive organ anatomy & function · STIs/RTIs and fertility impact · myths about fertility

## Tooba — Vaginal & Personal Hygiene

**Covers:** routine intimate hygiene practices, normal vs. abnormal vaginal discharge, odor, itching, vaginal/urinary infections (yeast infections, bacterial vaginosis, UTIs) not framed around fertility, general genital-area skin care, hygiene product use (soaps, wipes, underwear).

**Excludes → goes elsewhere:**
- Hygiene specifically during menstruation (pad/cloth changing) → *Menstrual Health & Periods*
- Infections framed around conception/fertility impact → *Fertility & Reproductive Health*
- Postpartum hygiene/discharge (lochia) → *Pregnancy & Maternal Health*

**Suggested sub-clusters:** daily intimate hygiene basics · normal vs. abnormal discharge · odor & itching causes · yeast infections & bacterial vaginosis · UTIs · hygiene products & practices · myths & taboos around vaginal health

## Mahnoor — Women's Nutrition & Wellness

**Covers:** general diet and nutrition across life stages (not pregnancy- or menopause-specific), anaemia and iron intake generally, physical activity/exercise, weight management (non-PCOS), sleep and rest, general preventive care and common physical ailments (fatigue, headaches, minor illness), hydration.

**Excludes → goes elsewhere:**
- Pregnancy-specific nutrition (iron-folic acid in pregnancy, foods to avoid while pregnant) → *Pregnancy & Maternal Health*
- Menopause-specific nutrition/bone health → *Menopause & Menopausal Health*
- Weight/insulin issues tied specifically to PCOS → *PCOS & Hormonal Health*
- Mental/emotional wellbeing → *Mental Health & Emotional Well-being*

**Suggested sub-clusters:** everyday balanced diet · anaemia & iron-rich foods · hydration & general wellness · physical activity & exercise · weight management (general) · sleep & rest · common everyday ailments · preventive health check-ups

---

## Mahnoor — Menopause & Menopausal Health

**Covers:** perimenopause and menopause symptoms (hot flashes, night sweats, irregular/absent periods due to age-related hormonal change), bone health and osteoporosis risk, menopause-specific nutrition, managing menopause symptoms, post-menopause health screening, hormone changes at menopause.

**Excludes → goes elsewhere:**
- Irregular periods in younger women or with suspected PCOS → *Menstrual Health & Periods* or *PCOS & Hormonal Health*
- General (non-menopause) nutrition → *Women's Nutrition & Wellness*
- Emotional/mood impact of menopause → *Mental Health & Emotional Well-being*

**Suggested sub-clusters:** perimenopause vs. menopause basics · common symptoms (hot flashes, night sweats, sleep) · periods during perimenopause · bone health & osteoporosis · menopause-specific nutrition · managing symptoms & when to seek care · post-menopause health screening · myths about menopause

## Shared rules across all 8 subtopics
- Every entry follows the existing danger-signs hard stop: if a question describes a danger sign, the answer defers to escalation, not a normal FAQ answer.
- Every entry carries the same "general information only, not a diagnosis — see a lady health worker/doctor" caveat already used in `kb_womens_health_sindhi.md`.
- Format matches `kb_rag_sindhi.md`: Sindhi question, English gloss, Sindhi answer, English gloss, source reference.
- When in doubt about which subtopic owns a question, check this doc first — don't guess and duplicate.
